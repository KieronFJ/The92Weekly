Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

The92Weekly ("The92Weekly", "we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit the92weekly.com, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

In short: The92Weekly is a football media website. We do not ask you to provide personal information simply to browse the site. Where third-party advertising or similar services use cookies or other identifiers, you can manage your choices through the consent controls presented on the site.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you use the site, information may include:

  • Technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, approximate location derived from an IP address, and pages visited.
  • Cookie and similar technology information used for functionality, security, analytics or advertising, where applicable.
  • Information you choose to send to us, for example if you contact us by email.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through the normal operation of the website.

2. Cookies and similar technologies

The92Weekly may use cookies, local storage and similar technologies. Some may be necessary for the site to function, while others may be used by third-party services such as advertising providers.

Where consent is required, the site uses Google's consent-management tools to present choices to visitors. You can use the consent controls provided on the site to accept, reject or manage applicable advertising and other non-essential technologies.

3. Google AdSense and advertising

The92Weekly may display advertising through Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, advertising identifiers or similar technologies to provide, measure and personalise advertising, subject to the choices and legal requirements that apply to you.

For users in regions where consent is required, advertising-related processing is controlled through the consent choices presented by our consent-management platform.

Google's privacy practices are described in Google's own privacy documentation. You can also manage certain Google advertising preferences through Google's advertising settings.

4. Third-party services

The site uses third-party services to support its operation and content, which may process technical information when your browser requests resources from them. These can include:

  • Google services, including Google AdSense and Google-hosted fonts.
  • ESPN data services, used to provide football fixtures, results and related competition data. The football information itself does not require us to collect personal information from you.
  • Other third-party services may be added in the future. Where a service materially changes how personal information is processed, this policy may be updated.

5. How we use information

Information available to us may be used to:

  • operate, maintain and secure The92Weekly;
  • understand website usage and improve the site's content and functionality;
  • display and measure advertising where applicable;
  • respond to enquiries sent to us; and
  • comply with legal obligations and protect the site against abuse or fraud.

6. Legal bases for processing

Where UK or EU data-protection law applies, the legal basis for processing will depend on the activity. This may include your consent, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the website, performance of a service you request, or compliance with a legal obligation.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help us operate the website, provide advertising, maintain security, or deliver other website functionality. Those providers process information according to their own privacy policies and the arrangements applicable to their services.

8. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to maintain records where required, or to meet legal and security requirements. Retention periods can vary depending on the type of information and the service involved.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live and which data-protection law applies, you may have rights including access to personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction of processing, objection to certain processing, and withdrawal of consent.

If you have concerns about how your personal information is handled, you can contact us using the details below. You may also have the right to complain to the relevant data-protection authority, including the UK's Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

10. Children's privacy

The92Weekly is a general football website and is not directed specifically at children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the normal browsing experience.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when our website, advertising arrangements or legal obligations change. The latest version will always be published on this page, with the date shown at the top.

12. Contact

If you have a privacy question or request, contact The92Weekly at hello@92weekly.com.
