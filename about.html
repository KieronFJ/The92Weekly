About

Most football coverage stops at the Premier League's edge. This doesn't.

92 Weekly covers English football across all four divisions — Premier League, Championship, League One, League Two — as one connected system, not four separate stories.

The national press has six Premier League clubs on repeat and treats everything below it as an afterthought. But that's not where football actually happens for most of the country. It's where players get built before they're ready for the top flight, where relegated clubs either rebuild properly or spiral, and where the financial gap between divisions tells you more about the modern game than another transfer rumour ever will.

This is a weekly publication for people who already know the basics and want the version of football writing that connects the dots — how a Championship recruitment model predicts a Premier League one two seasons later, why a League Two forward is worth watching now, what a club's wage bill actually says about their season before ball is kicked.

What This Is

  • 01

    Every division treated as one story

    Promotion, relegation, loan pipelines, recruitment patterns — followed across tiers, not siloed by league.

  • 02

    Depth over speed

    No live-blogging transfer deadline day. One weekly send, written properly, with the analysis the daily news cycle skips.

  • 03

    Written by a fan, not a wire service

    An Ipswich Town supporter's-eye view of English football — genuine lower-league affection, not a parachuted-in national angle.
